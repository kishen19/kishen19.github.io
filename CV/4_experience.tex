    \emph{Manager:} Rajesh Jayaram, Graph Mining Team
    \par

We developed a practical Lloyd-style iterative approach, \emph{MV-Lloyd}, for clustering point clouds and building high-quality multi-vector cluster centers. We leverage this to design \emph{MV-IVF}, an IVF-based method for highly-scalable multi-vector retrieval.
